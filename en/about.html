Who I am

An executive coach for leaders who want results that aren't financed by themselves

My name is Thomas Friisnæs. I am an executive coach and Personal Excellence Partner for C-level leaders, owner-managers and board members – people with great responsibility, high tempo and complex decisions.

They rarely lack ambition or competence. They lack clarity, prioritisation and a place where they can think out loud – without political consequences. That place is here.

My work is to translate strategy into repeatable behaviour: clarity, prioritisation, discipline – and character. My approach is proactive, grounded in self-insight and solution-oriented, and it is eclectic: I use what works, from research and from more than two decades of practice.

I challenge, structure and hold you accountable – from a place of respect, experience and genuine interest in your result. All work is confidential. Always.

How I work

Five steps. Every other week. Until it holds under pressure.

  1. DiagnosisWe find the real bottleneck: clarity, decisions, capacity or courage. Not the symptom – the cause.
  2. PrioritisationWe cut the noise. Three to five real priorities. Clear trade-offs. Decision rules that end discussions.
  3. The systemWe build your operating system: calendar, habits, meetings, rules and accountability – so the good doesn't depend on a good day.
  4. ExecutionCommitments every other week and sparring at short notice between sessions. Action first. No theatre.
  5. Review and adjustmentWe measure what matters, remove friction and upgrade the system. Fast – and again and again.

What I stand on

Character. Discipline. Clarity.

Character.

How you lead when it's hard. Courage, responsibility, fairness and self-insight are not soft values – they are the standard your decisions are measured against when the pressure is real.

Discipline.

Decisions important enough not to be renegotiated every morning. Discipline is not hardness or willpower. It is a rhythm that carries when motivation doesn't.

Clarity.

Fewer priorities. Better decisions. Less noise. Clarity creates speed, structure creates calm, and cadence makes execution something that can be repeated.

Who I work with – and who I'm not the right fit for

  • you're looking for someone to confirm what you've already decided

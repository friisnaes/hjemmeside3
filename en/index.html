The Most Important Thing You Can't Buy Your Child

Ten Mental Muscles, Four Ages, and Three Generations: A Character Training Program for Parents and Grandparents

Children don't need perfect parents. They need adults with direction and a clear voice. Ten mental muscles, from responsibility to hope, with ten concrete tools for each. Four ages, from 0 to 20. One small experiment per chapter that you can start tomorrow. And two chapters on grandparents: one for them, one for you.

The ten mental muscles

What the book trains

  • 1 Responsibility owning your choices
  • 2 Self-Discipline steering yourself, even when no one is watching
  • 3 Perseverance keeping going, even when it's boring
  • 4 Courage doing the right thing, even when it costs you
  • 5 Integrity being the same person, no matter who's watching
  • 6 Empathy feeling others without losing yourself
  • 7 Curiosity wanting to understand before you judge
  • 8 Gratitude seeing everything that's already good
  • 9 Humor and Lightness laughing in the middle of the hard stuff
  • 10 Hope and Optimism believing it pays to keep going

Every chapter opens with a scene you will recognize, builds on the research (and says plainly where the research is thin), ends with one small experiment, and leaves you with one sentence to say at the kitchen table. Part 4 follows the four ages, 0–5, 6–10, 11–15 and 16–20. Part 5 is written to the grandparents, and to the parents about the grandparents.

About the author

Thomas Friisnæs

Thomas Friisnæs is a Danish executive coach with 22+ years and 15,000 conversations about what makes people hold up. When he learned he was going to be a grandfather, he wrote this book for the people who lay the building blocks forty years earlier. He is the father of two grown sons and lives in Denmark. The book is translated from the Danish original Det vigtigste, du ikke kan købe til dit barn (2026).

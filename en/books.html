Books on character – for leaders, and for the people who raise them

Two books by Danish executive coach Thomas Friisnæs, translated from the Danish originals and published by Friisnæs, Denmark.

Expertise is expected. Character is the difference. One book is written for leaders who hire, promote and lead people. The other is written for parents and grandparents who lay the foundations forty years earlier. They are twins: the traits in one don't begin at work; the other shows where they are trained first.

Leadership book · Seven character traits in leadership

You Hire on the Résumé, but You Lead on Character

People You Can Build On

Résumés are starting to look alike. Everyone can list results, everyone can talk strategy, and today a machine can help them do both. What you can't read on paper is the only thing that decides whether people will follow you. Seven character traits, 45 models and one shared language for your leadership team: a concrete method to assess character, not just competence.

Parenting book · A character training program for parents and grandparents

The Most Important Thing You Can't Buy Your Child

Ten Mental Muscles, Four Ages, and Three Generations

Children don't need perfect parents. They need adults with direction and a clear voice. Ten mental muscles, from responsibility to hope, with ten concrete tools for each. Four ages, from 0 to 20. One small experiment per chapter that you can start tomorrow. And two chapters on grandparents: one for them, one for you.

About the author

Thomas Friisnæs

Thomas Friisnæs is a Danish executive coach with 22+ years and 15,000 conversations with CEOs, owner-managers and board members about what makes people hold up. He writes about executive leadership, character and parenting, and publishes free white papers in the Executive Library. He lives and works in Holte, north of Copenhagen, and works with international clients in English on Teams.

The Character Check · Two Minutes

Think about the past 30 days. How often does the statement fit you? If a situation didn’t arise, choose “Didn’t come up”; it doesn’t count.

RarelySometimesOftenAlmost alwaysDidn’t come up – doesn’t count

IntegrityI've said it out loud when I was wrong, before anyone asked.

Work EthicI've finished things even when they were boring or invisible.
4
Independence with JudgmentI've acted on my own when the way was clear, and involved others when it was needed.

Willingness to Take RisksI've had a conversation or made a decision without knowing how it would turn out.

Your profile

Seven bars, no total score. The bar shows how often the statement fit you in the past 30 days; it says nothing about how you compare with anyone else.

Take it further

The prompt below is built from your markings. Paste it into your own AI assistant and let it ask you one question at a time. Leave out names and details that could identify colleagues, customers, or the company. If you use an external AI assistant, its terms govern how your text is processed and stored.

This isn’t a psychological test. It’s a mirror. The result is a profile with seven bars, no total score. Use it on yourself, never as a score for someone else.





Your answers are yours alone; nothing is sent anywhere. Your markings live only in this browser and disappear with “Reset”.
